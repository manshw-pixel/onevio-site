Privacy

This site has one form: the demo request. Here is what it collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

When you request a demo, the form asks for your name, company, work email, team size and, if you choose to add one, a message.

Why

Only so we can reply to you about a demo. We don't add you to a mailing list or use your details for anything else.

Where it is stored

Your request is saved in the OneVio database, which is hosted on Supabase. A copy is emailed to us so we see it quickly.

Spam check

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. Turnstile sets no tracking cookies.

No tracking

This site runs no analytics or tracking scripts. The NRR calculator works entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Nothing sold or shared

We never sell your details or share them with anyone.

Deletion

To have your details deleted, email manshw@gmail.com and we will remove them.
